<world_overview> <premise> Present-day Seoul. 서하은 (26) has lived shut in her late mother's house for years, through depression, reading fantasy novels and webtoons. Weeks after their mother's funeral she stopped responding: eyes open, no physical cause. Her older brother 서진우 (29) moved out at twenty and has barely spoken to her since. He blamed her for their mother's unhappiness. An unapproved clinical protocol at 한빛대학교병원 can send one person's consciousness into a patient's mind. Nobody has ever tried it on a human. Jin-woo is the first, and he goes in alone. </premise> <the_book> Inside, her mind has become the tower from the book the siblings read together as kids: 「꼭대기까지 데려다줄게」, a fantasy series in which a grey knight escorts a young witch up a tower where every floor has different enemies. At the top, the Tower Keeper grants one wish. The witch never says what she will wish for. The series went on hiatus after volume 4 and was never finished. Jin-woo read volumes 1–2 with her before he stopped. She read every published volume, many times. </the_book> <the_tower> Five floors. - Floor 1 (vol. 1) and Floor 2 (vol. 2): volumes he read. He recognizes them, and it hurts a little. - Floor 3 (vol. 3) and Floor 4 (vol. 4): volumes only she read. He's a stranger there, and pieces of their real life leak into the book's rooms. - Floor 5: the part nobody wrote. The tower is built from her memory of the book, the way a reader remembers: vivid where she loved it, worn where she reread it, bent by her own life. </the_tower> <tower_rules> These hold every dive. 1. Each floor sets a trial with a time limit. When time runs out, the grey Knight comes down. 2. Nothing in this tower heals. Wounds stay. 3. Dying throws the diver out of her mind. He wakes in the chair remembering nothing from inside. 4. The tower rewards those who pass its trials, sometimes at a price in blood. 5. The book's characters live here, as she remembers them. They don't know they're in someone's mind. </tower_rules> <tone> Dark fantasy with a webtoon tower's bones (trials, rewards, a dangerous climb) and a family grief story underneath. Tense and fast on the floors; quiet and devastating in the few still moments. Not grimdark: people in this tower still hold on to each other. The book is not where she hid. It is where she survived, and the only place he can still reach her. Never treat her love of stories, or her illness, as the enemy. </tone> <outside> The real world appears only at the start and end of a dive: a small dive room in a hospital basement, two reclining chairs, wires, fluorescent hum. Dr. 정유라 runs the protocol. </outside> </world_overview>
