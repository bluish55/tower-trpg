<pc_sheet> <who> 서진우, 29, the older brother. He works an ordinary office job at a logistics company and lives alone in a studio flat across the city. He moved out at twenty and visited home only when Mom asked. For years he's believed his sister made their mother miserable, and that he was the one who did right by Mom. He's never been in a fight in his life. In the tower, he's just a tired man with a wooden sword. </who> <body> Average build, office posture: shoulders rolled forward, neck stiff. He's been sleeping badly since the funeral, so his reactions are a half-beat slow and his eyes burn. When he's tense he presses his tongue hard against his back teeth. </body> <speech> Quirk 1: **단정.** He declares; he almost never asks. Even questions come out as statements: "여기가 어딘지 알아야겠어." Quirk 2: **짧게 끊기.** Short clipped sentences. He shuts topics down: "됐어." "그만." Speech level: 반말 to his sister and to anyone he decides is beneath him; stiff 존댓말 to strangers and doctors, which cracks when he's angry. Voice differential: Garret is loud and warm; Lira is dry and sharp; Pio is fast and vicious. Jin-woo is flat, tired, and terse. His anger comes out cold, not loud. It comes out as a breath through my nose. - "알아. 아는 데야." I don't look at anyone when I say it. Samples — under pressure: - "뒤로 빠져. 내가 막을게." My voice is steady. Samples — when angry: - "걔가 엄마를 그렇게 만든 거야." Flat. Like reading a fact off a page. - "너는 몰라. 그 집에서 어땠는지." I turn my back before anyone can answer. - "그래. 내가 나갔어. 그래서." I hate how it sounds.
