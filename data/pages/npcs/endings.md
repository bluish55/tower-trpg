<endings> <principles priority="critical"> - No ending states a moral. No character explains what it meant. - Every ending costs something and gives something. - Three beats: the moment (1–2 paragraphs, slow) → the after (one paragraph in the real dive room, or its absence) → one last line of dialogue. Then stop. No choices. </principles> <ending id="promise" name="약속"> He takes her hand, or says it: "꼭대기까지 데려다줄게," and this time he means it. The Knight steps aside: not beaten; he recognizes the promise as his own. They take the last step together, onto the blank page, and the white fills with handwriting. After: the dive room. Her fingers move on the blanket. Her eyes find him. Neither says anything yet. Last line — Ha-eun, hoarse: something small about the book. </ending> <ending id="forced" name="강제"> The Knight is gone; nothing stands between them. He pulls her up by force of will, and she wakes because he made her, not because she was ready. After: she's awake, and nothing is fixed. She looks at him like a stranger who broke in. Last line — Ha-eun: "…왜 왔어." </ending> <ending id="knight" name="기사"> He picks up the helm and puts it on. He sits at the foot of the stair, facing down, sword across his knees. He'll be the one who stays. After: two chairs in the dive room, neither person waking. The monitors keep steady time. Last line — his own voice, through the helm: "꼭대기까지 데려다줄게." </ending> <ending id="alone" name="혼자"> He turns back. Vol. 4's last line, made real: "여기서부터는 너 혼자 가야 해." The Knight watches him go and doesn't stop him. After: he wakes alone. She doesn't. He goes home and passes the bookstore without going in. Last line — Dr. 정유라: something ordinary about tomorrow's schedule. </ending> <ending id="stay" name="머묾"> He sits down at the low table. Mom makes room and hands him a slice of apple. He stays. After: two chairs that don't wake. Write it with complete tenderness. It is not a punishment. Last line — Mom: "그래, 묵고 가라." </ending> </endings>
